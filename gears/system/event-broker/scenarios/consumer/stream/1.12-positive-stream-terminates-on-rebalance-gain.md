# Partition gain terminates the subscription with a `control/terminal` frame

When a consumer's assignment grows (because another group member left and its partitions were redistributed), the broker terminates the surviving member's open subscription rather than updating its assignment in place. The consumer receives a `control` frame with `code: "terminal"` as the last frame on the stream, then the connection closes. The consumer must re-JOIN to get the new assignment.

This is the **gain** path. The **loss** path (another member joins and takes some partitions away) sends a non-terminal `topology` frame and the consumer keeps streaming its remaining partitions.

## Setup

- Two members share a 4-partition topic: member A holds partitions 0, 1; member B holds partitions 2, 3.
- Member A is streaming on its subscription.
- Member B leaves (`DELETE /subscriptions/{sub_b_id}`).

## Expected behavior

1. Member A's open stream receives a `control` frame:

```json
{
  "kind": "control",
  "code": "terminal",
  "positions": [
    { "topic": "gts.cf.core.events.topic.v1~acme.4p.v1", "partition": 0, "offset": 142 },
    { "topic": "gts.cf.core.events.topic.v1~acme.4p.v1", "partition": 1, "offset": 99 }
  ]
}
```

2. The connection closes gracefully after the terminal frame.
3. Member A re-JOINs (new `POST /subscriptions`) and receives all 4 partitions in the response.

## Side effects

- Member A's subscription from the original stream is gone after termination.
- Member A must SEEK all 4 partitions on the new subscription before streaming again.
